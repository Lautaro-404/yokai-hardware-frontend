## Brand & Style

The brand marries ancient Japanese artisanal mastery—specifically the gold leaf (*kinpaku*), mineral pigments, and rich sumi ink brushwork of classical Byōbu screens—with high-precision contemporary computing hardware and peripheral engineering. It addresses enthusiasts, engineers, and digital craftsmen who demand uncompromising build quality alongside deep cultural resonance and tactile poetry.

The design style is a calibrated synthesis of **Tactile Artistry** and **Modern Architectural Precision**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered tonal backdrops reminiscent of aged mulberry parchment (*washi*), antique brass leafing, and charred lacquer (*shou sugi ban*).
- **Decisive Calligraphic Weight:** Deliberate hierarchy marked by fine hairline frame rules, metallic luster accents, and deliberate negative space (*ma*).
- **Subtle Organic Contrast:** Precision industrial typography anchored by natural mineral vermilion accents (*shu-iro*) and deep evergreen pine moss (*tokiwa-iro*).

## Colors

The palette directly references the imperial screens, ink washes, and mineral pigments of historical Japanese screen painting:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Kinpaku Gold):** Represents antique gilded cloud-layers and machined brass hardware surfaces. Used for primary interactive actions, high-tier status elements, active borders, and crucial highlight telemetry.
- **Secondary (`#C93B2B` - Shinku Vermilion):** Sourced from lacquered torii gates, shrine woodwork, and spring plum blossoms. Employed sparingly for urgent system alerts, active states, key conversion triggers, and artisan emblem marks.
- **Tertiary (`#3E5848` - Tokiwa Pine):** Deep mossy green derived from ancient pine needles and mountain slopes in the screen panels. Used for secondary tags, successful telemetry readings, and peripheral status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1A1815` - Sumi Charcoal):** Rich, ink-ground dark foundation replacing generic tech blacks with the warm, velvety undertone of dry pine soot ink. Accompanying surface containers step up through aged parchment taupe (`#24201B`), smoked iron (`#332D26`), and soft golden bone parchment (`#E8DFD0`) for high-legibility display text.

## Typography

The pairing reflects the tension between classical art history and modern hardware craftsmanship:

- **Headlines (Noto Serif):** Delivers the stately cadence of woodblock title strips and imperial manuscripts. Headings carry generous vertical leading and purposeful, measured rhythm.
- **Body & Technical Labels (Space Grotesk):** Provides engineered legibility, technical geometric clarity, and monoline cleanliness necessary for hardware metrics, spec sheets, and high-density dashboards.

Letter spacing on all upper-case badges and metric tags should be set to `0.08em` to mimic precision CNC-engraved hardware labeling.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to the concept of **Ma** (negative space as a deliberate design entity) translated into an adaptive 12-column architectural grid:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Generous outer margins (`2.5rem`) with `1.5rem` gutters. Hardware modules and specifications snap to structural groupings mimicking the folding panels of a multi-panel Byōbu screen.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product schematics re-stack into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Dense hardware metrics shift into horizontal swipeable modules, preserving continuous baseline alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of metallic luster boundaries and sumi-wash ambient depth:

- **Base Canvas:** Deep sumi matte texture (`#1A1815`) simulating ink-saturated washi.
- **Mid-Tier (Panels & Enclosures):** Surface containers elevated with fine 1px antique gold borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`) and soft inner ambient gradients tilting slightly toward smoked bronze.
- **High-Tier (Flyouts & Active Dialogs):** Rich, semi-translucent charcoal glass (`rgba(26, 24, 21, 0.88)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient, warm gold glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The form factor reflects the crisp, disciplined edges of joinery (*sashimono*) and machined anodized aluminum. Elements use tight, deliberate radii (4px base, 8px large) that maintain a grounded, architectural quality without feeling aggressive or strictly brutalist. Overly round or pill-shaped geometries are strictly forbidden to honor the structured paneling of Japanese folding screens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid antique gold fill (`#D4AF37`) with dark sumi-charcoal text (`#1A1815`). Crisp 4px radius, hover transition introduces a delicate vermilion edge glow (`0 0 12px rgba(201, 59, 43, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with a 1px antique brass border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) and warm parchment text (`#E8DFD0`). On hover, fills with subtle bronze translucency (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Destructive / Alert:** Muted cinnabar red fill (`#C93B2B`) with bone parchment typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Dark sumi-tier backdrop (`#151311`) framed with a 1px subtle bronze stroke (`#3D352B`). Focus transitions the border to active gold (`#D4AF37`) with a 2px offset ambient halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted square or diamond-faceted silhouettes. Checked state displays a vibrant cinnabar stamp glyph reminiscent of an artisan seal (*hanko*).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Low-saturation pine moss (`#3E5848`) or muted amber leaf bases framed with hairline gold borders, housing compact, uppercase monospace technical telemetry.

### Cards & Hardware Displays
- **Panels:** Designed as individual folding screen leaves. Feature hairline divider lines, quiet dark surfaces, and subtle corner registration marks (`+` or `L`-brackets) that recall both technical hardware blueprints and traditional woodblock printing registration marks (*kento*).
